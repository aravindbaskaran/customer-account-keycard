# Code of conduct

Be kind, play nice, and be articulate.

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work.
- Assume good intent, ask questions, and own mistakes.
- Do not harass, insult, discriminate, threaten, or share someone else's
  private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not meet these standards.
Report conduct concerns privately to cestmoiaravind@gmail.com.
